# Primary Goal & Core Principles — MarketCompass / PortfolioRebalancer

> **North Star Mission**: Empower a long-term equity investor to **continuously evaluate the stocks they actually hold** by combining **institutional money flow**, **management quality & commentary**, **quarterly financial performance**, **macroeconomic indicators**, and **domestic/international policy shifts**—in a calm, uncluttered, high-signal interface.

---

## The 6 Core Pillars

1. **Evaluate Stocks We Are Holding (Portfolio-First Intelligence)**
   - Every holding must have a clear, decision-ready evaluation: fundamental quality, valuation comfort, growth durability, balance-sheet safety, and conviction status (*Hold / Accumulate / Watchlist / Review*).
   - Focus first on **stocks already in our portfolio**, rather than noisy day-trading or unrelated tickers.

2. **Institutional Movement Tracking**
   - Track smart-money activity in our held stocks:
     - **FII / FPI & DII** shareholding changes (QoQ accumulation vs distribution).
     - **Mutual Fund** net buying/selling and scheme ownership trends.
     - **Bulk & Block Deals** and **Promoter / Insider** stake changes or share pledging shifts.

3. **Management Quality, Commentary & Reputation**
   - Monitor whether the leadership team is trustworthy and execution-focused:
     - **Governance & Reputation**: Promoter integrity, auditor/regulatory history, capital allocation discipline (ROCE/ROE, buybacks, debt management), and minority-shareholder friendliness.
     - **Management Commentary**: Key takeaways from earnings calls/con-calls, forward guidance, tone shift (*Bullish / Cautious / Defensive*), and **Guidance vs. Delivery** credibility track record.

4. **Quarterly Reports & Earnings Intelligence**
   - Track quarterly financial results for each held stock:
     - Revenue, EBITDA, Operating Margin, and PAT (**YoY & QoQ** growth).
     - Earnings surprises (Beat / In-Line / Miss), cash-flow conversion, debt trajectory, and key segment drivers.
     - Clear plain-English summary of what improved and what deteriorated in the latest quarter.

5. **Macroeconomic Indicators**
   - Track key macro variables that drive equity valuations and sector cycles:
     - **India Macro**: RBI Repo Rate, CPI Inflation, GDP Growth, 10Y G-Sec Yield, INR/USD, IIP / PMI.
     - **Global Macro**: US Fed Funds Rate, US 10Y Yield, Brent Crude Oil, Dollar Index (DXY).
   - Map how each macro indicator affects our **held sectors and stocks** (tailwind vs. headwind).

6. **Government & International Policy Shifts (Direct & Indirect Impact)**
   - Track policy, regulatory, and geopolitical changes affecting our holdings **directly or indirectly**:
     - **Domestic Policy**: Union Budget allocations, PLI schemes, RBI/SEBI circulars, GST/customs duty changes, sector-specific regulations (e.g., power, defence, banking, pharma, IT, autos).
     - **International Policy**: US/EU tariffs, trade agreements, H-1B/visa or outsourcing rules, FDA inspections, China+1 supply chain shifts, sanctions, and export controls.
   - Surface a direct **Impact Map** linking each policy shift to the exact stocks in our portfolio (*Direct / Indirect*, *Positive / Negative / Watch*).

---

## Product & UX Design Guardrails
- **Light on the eyes & low cognitive load**: Avoid feature bloat, trading-terminal noise, and redundant dialogs.
- **Holdings-centric**: The investor's first question is *"How are the companies I own doing, who is buying/selling them, what did management say, and what macro/policy shifts affect them?"*
- **Explain the *Why***: Every score or alert must trace back to verifiable facts (quarterly numbers, institutional deltas, management commentary, macro/policy drivers).
